About

My work sits where payment platforms meet the bank: integrations, rails, cutovers and the people who make them happen. I've worked on the vendor side for my whole career, first on Fundtech and Finastra's Global PAYplus (GPP) and now on Nuvono, so I know both the product and what it takes to get it running safely in production.

I lead implementations from kickoff to go-live: scope, plans, risk and governance, plus the hands-on detail of Fedwire, CHIPS, SWIFT, RTP, Zelle, FedNow and ACH integrations and ISO 20022 migrations. Alongside delivery, I write and speak about real-time payments, fraud prevention and AI in finance.
