Payments Modernization Hub

Java★ 141

A reference payments hub showing how a bank can run real-time and high-value payments through one ISO 20022-based platform.

  • Payment engine, orchestrator and multi-rail router, with idempotency and replay detection.
  • RTP and Zelle services for limits, fees, alias validation and enrollment.
  • pacs.008 parsing and pacs.002 status building, plus fraud, velocity, cut-off and country-risk rules.
  • Ledger posting, clearing windows, cutover configuration and feature toggles.

Real-Time Payment Architecture & Orchestration

Java★ 67

A framework for instant-payment rails (RTP, FedNow, SEPA Instant) that walks a credit transfer from validation to real-time acknowledgment.

  • ISO 20022 data models for pacs.008, pacs.009, pacs.002 and pain.001.
  • Pluggable validation, enrichment, routing and fraud evaluation in a Kafka-ready pipeline.
  • Resilience and observability patterns: circuit breakers, dead-letter queues, retries and correlation IDs.
  • Architecture, sequence and routing-decision diagrams.
